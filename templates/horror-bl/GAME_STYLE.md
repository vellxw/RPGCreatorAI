# La Novena Promesa — style bible

`GAME_STYLE.md` is the visual and sensory source of truth for this template.
Visible assets must follow it and carry provenance in `ASSETS.md` before they
can be approved.

## Direction

- Art mode: Anime HD dark, painterly backgrounds, clean readable portraits.
- Camera: top-down exploration at 16:9 with fixed-room framing; illustrated VN
  panels for memories and choices.
- Palette: wet graphite `#11151D`, surgical teal `#5AC8D8`, warning amber
  `#E4A24A`, blood crimson `#A62F45`, text bone `#F1E8D6`.
- Lighting: motivated fluorescent pools, black corners, restrained bloom.
- Characters: adults with distinct silhouettes, hands and feet readable in all
  four directions, shared ground-contact anchor.
- Motion: idle breathing, 8 fps walk, short holds before horror reveals;
  no camera shake during choices or subtitles.
- UI: compact dark panels, large speaker labels, never communicate danger by
  hue alone; pair color with icon, value, or text.

## Horror and romance rules

Body horror is suggestive and legible, not a wall of noise: show cause,
reaction, and consequence. Romance is adult, mutual, and character-driven;
the horror must not excuse coercion. No fetishization of injury or identity.

## Asset prohibitions

Do not copy Gorest demo assets, logos, characters, or branding. Do not use
unlicensed likenesses, voices, references, or remote URLs. AI candidates remain
quarantined until a human reviews anatomy, continuity, consent, license, and
runtime readability.
